The last building before the water.

Torreguadiaro is the quiet beach village at the eastern edge of Sotogrande, where the marina gives way to a long stretch of sand, a handful of chiringuitos and a promenade that runs for miles. This apartment sits on the seventh floor of a front-line building, so every principal room opens towards the sea rather than the street.

Inside, the layout is simple and generous: a wide living and dining room that flows onto the terrace, a separate fitted kitchen, three true double bedrooms and two bathrooms. It works equally well as a permanent home, a winter base or a lock-up-and-leave summer apartment ten minutes from the polo fields and the golf courses of Sotogrande.

Torreguadiaro and Sotogrande

A beach village with a world-class resort behind it.

Torreguadiaro keeps the easy rhythm of a Cádiz beach town: a morning swim, coffee on the promenade, fish at a chiringuito with your feet almost in the sand. Walk west and within fifteen minutes you reach the marina at Puerto Sotogrande, with its restaurants, yacht club and Sunday market.

Behind the beach lies one of Europe's most complete sporting estates. Valderrama, Real Club de Golf Sotogrande, La Reserva and Almenara are all within a ten-minute drive, the polo season at Santa María Polo Club fills the summer, and the international schools make this a year-round community rather than a seasonal one.

Gibraltar airport is twenty minutes away, with direct flights to London, Manchester, Bristol and Edinburgh. Málaga is just over an hour up the coast.
